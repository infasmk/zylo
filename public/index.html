📲 How to install as an iOS App

  1. Open this website in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner.

Once installed, Bloomee runs in full-screen standalone mode with background audio playback and native lock screen controls!
